Coverage

What “known” means: public data can be incomplete, stale, or wrong. A place with no displayed record is not guaranteed to be camera-free or surveillance-free.

Why coverage matters

More awareness.
Better choices.

WayVeil is built around a simple idea: drivers should be able to understand the privacy context of a route without pretending the data knows more than it does.

01

Surveillance is real.

ALPR systems can be part of everyday roads and intersections. WayVeil makes supported known records visible instead of leaving that context buried.

02

Route with awareness.

Known exposure is one factor WayVeil can use when comparing supported routes, alongside the practical job of getting you where you need to go.

03

Coverage expands carefully.

New regions should appear only when their navigation package and surveillance-intelligence state can be described honestly and supported responsibly.

Truth over theater

No marker does not mean no surveillance.

WayVeil reports what the current supported public snapshot knows. Unknown cameras can exist, data changes, and surveillance intelligence may be incomplete even where navigation coverage is available.
