One more question

Normal navigation asks what’s fastest.
WayVeil asks what you’ll pass.

WayVeil keeps expected navigation fundamentals, then adds a privacy-aware view of known ALPR exposure. You choose how much that matters for each trip.

FASTEST

Get there sooner.

Use the fastest supported route when time matters more than known exposure.

BALANCED

Make the tradeoff visible.

Compare a route that can reduce known exposure without pretending privacy has no time cost.

ZERO KNOWN ALPR

Choose zero known when it exists.

If a true zero-known route is unavailable, WayVeil says Best Available instead of faking certainty.

Privacy by architecture

Your route is not your account.

For Cohort One, alpha access proves only that a tester is authorized. It does not become a navigation identity and does not receive your private route history, raw GPS, destination history, or Private Session state.
