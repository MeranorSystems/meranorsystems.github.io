WayVeil/Privacy

Privacy by architecture

Your route is not your account.

Cohort-One access and private navigation are intentionally separate. The alpha layer may establish that you are an authorized tester; it is not allowed to become the identifier for your routes, GPS fixes, destinations, searches, or Private Session activity.

What stays on the navigation side of the wall.

The following private navigation content is outside the Cohort-One entitlement scope.

G

Raw GPS

The access layer is not authorized to receive raw location fixes or location trails.

R

Routes & destinations

Origin/destination, route geometry, route history, and navigation-session history are not alpha-account data.

P

Privacy activity

Private Session state and VeilScore / ALPR encounter history do not become tester-profile fields.

LOCAL_GUEST

No cloud navigation identity required.

For Cohort One, LOCAL_GUEST remains the accepted navigation model. Revoking alpha entitlement must not rewrite your local navigation profile into a cloud-account model or erase your local data.

✓Entitlement: “this tester may use the closed alpha.”
✓Navigation: stays local-first and separate.
✓Active guidance: access problems must not become a remote kill switch while you are moving.

What the public website does today.

W5 remains a presentation/mocked-portal layer. The alpha-interest form is deliberately non-live until collection behavior and the hosted access contract are separately accepted.

Analytics

No marketing analytics are enabled by default.

Alpha form

Preview only in W5. Submitting it sends no network request.

Secrets

No privileged service secret may be embedded in JavaScript, static assets, manifests, or other client-readable bundles.

Future collection

Any live signup, diagnostics, sync, or account behavior requires a separate decision that describes exactly what is collected and why.
