What you’ll do

Real navigation. Real roads. Real feedback.

The first Alpha is about learning how WayVeil behaves outside the lab — without turning drivers into passive data sources.

Navigate with WayVeil

Use the accepted WayVeil driving experience in supported areas and tell us where navigation feels strong — or confusing.

Test privacy-aware routes

Compare Fastest, Balanced, and Zero Known ALPR / Best Available choices and flag anything that does not make sense.

Put it through real life

Help us learn how route guidance, rerouting, mobile behavior, voice guidance, and privacy context hold up in everyday use.

Tell us what needs work

Report confusing guidance, stale surveillance information, mobile issues, and anything that gets in the way of a good drive.

What we ask from testers

Help us make WayVeil better.

You do not need to be a developer. The most useful tester is somebody willing to use the product thoughtfully and tell us what actually happened.

Drive within supported areas.Use accepted regional coverage and keep the current coverage limits in mind.

Challenge the experience.Try route choices, rerouting, guidance, known-surveillance context, and normal everyday scenarios.

Tell us what you noticed.Useful feedback can be simple: what happened, what you expected, and whether it affected the drive.

Safety comes first

Drive first. Test second.

Mount the device, keep guidance glanceable, use audible guidance when needed, and leave setup, troubleshooting, updates, and detailed feedback for when you are parked.

Read Safety & Limitations

Access without driving identity

Your Alpha Pass is not your driving identity.

The Alpha access service only needs what is required to authorize the closed tester build. It does not receive raw GPS, origins or destinations, route geometry or history, trip history, known-ALPR encounter history, or Private Session state through that access layer.
