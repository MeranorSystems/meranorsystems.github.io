WayVeil/Safety

Closed-alpha safety

Set it up parked. Let guidance do the work.

Cohort One is an alpha, not a finished App Store release. Testers follow a deliberately narrow operating envelope so product feedback never competes with driving attention.

1

Before moving

  • Mount the device before departure.
  • Choose the destination and route while parked.
  • Confirm location permission, current data, and voice guidance before moving.
!

While moving

  • Do not type, search, report, troubleshoot, update, or reconfigure.
  • Keep the PWA foregrounded with the screen on in the accepted Cohort-One environment.
  • Use voice and glanceable guidance rather than hands-on interaction.
P

If something goes wrong

  • If guidance becomes unreliable, stop relying on it.
  • Pull over and park safely before troubleshooting or reauthorizing.
  • Use manual feedback after the drive rather than debugging on the road.
B

Current limitations

  • Background/lock-screen navigation is not a Cohort-One guarantee.
  • True downloadable offline visual regions are not yet a supported public claim.
  • Live traffic is not currently an accepted feature claim.
!

Privacy routing does not outrank safe driving.

A camera alert, VeilScore, route preference, alpha entitlement state, or data refresh should never pressure a tester to interact with the device while moving. Park first.

Access control is not a remote navigation kill switch.

If cohort access is revoked, expired, or temporarily unavailable, the distribution layer may block future sessions or require reauthorization when parked. It must not abruptly terminate active local guidance while a tester is moving.

✓Allowed: suspend new access, future sessions, or distribution of a known-bad release.
✓Required: active local guidance fails predictably and preserves local data.
✓Tester action: park before update, recovery, reauthentication, or diagnosis.
