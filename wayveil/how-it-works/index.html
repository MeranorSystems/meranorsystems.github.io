WayVeil/How It Works

Product

Navigation with privacy context built into the route choice.

WayVeil starts with familiar navigation: search, compare, navigate, reroute. Its difference is that known ALPR exposure becomes a visible routing factor instead of something you discover after the drive.

FastestBalancedZero Known ALPR / Best Available

Three steps, one extra layer of choice.

The privacy layer should add context without turning navigation into a cockpit.

1

Search

Choose a destination or place before driving, just like a normal navigation app.

2

Compare

Review supported route options with travel tradeoffs and known ALPR exposure made visible.

3

Navigate

Keep the next maneuver, route progress, hazards, and relevant known-exposure context glanceable while guidance runs locally.

Route modes

The names mean exactly what they say.

FASTEST

Time first.

Prioritizes travel time using the accepted routing model. It can still encounter known ALPR.

BALANCED

A visible compromise.

Can take a modest time tradeoff when doing so meaningfully reduces known exposure.

ZERO KNOWN ALPR

Zero known — when supported.

Targets zero encounters in the current known active dataset. If that is not available, the interface must say Best Available.

Local-first core

The website is not the routing brain.

WayVeil’s privacy-sensitive routing and navigation state are designed to remain local on the device wherever practical. The public site and alpha-access layer distribute and authorize the product; they do not become the navigation engine.
